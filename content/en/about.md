## Who I am

My name is **Nathan Charpentier**. I am building my career in **cybersecurity** with a structured, step-by-step approach.

I am particularly interested in how organisations **identify, manage and reduce their digital risks**, on both the technical and the organisational side.

---

## My view of cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is not only about tools or visible attacks.
It rests above all on:
- a **sound understanding of risks**
- **suitable preventive measures**
- **detection and response** capabilities
- the **involvement of users**

Technology, people and organisation have to work together.

---

## My professional direction

My profile is deliberately **generalist**: for now I am not committing to a single path.

My final-year internship had me practise **governance, risk and compliance (GRC)**, and my system-administration internship, **systems and networks**. I am also interested in **operational defence** (detection, incident handling) and in designing secure architectures, without wanting to limit myself to just one of these areas.

---

## What I am working on today

Through this site, I work on:
- **security watch** (technical, regulatory, operational)
- the **ability to summarise and explain clearly**
- **awareness** for non-technical audiences

This site is at once a **learning tool**, a **demonstration** and a **professional showcase**.
