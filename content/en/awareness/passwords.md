## A problem that is still current

Passwords are still the main way of protecting accounts.
Yet they remain one of the main entry points for attackers.

---

## The most common mistakes

- using the same password everywhere
- choosing a password that is too simple
- sharing credentials
- ignoring security alerts

These behaviours are human, but exploitable.

---

## What attackers do

Cybercriminals do not always try to "hack" technically:
- they test passwords that are already known
- they send fake e-mails (phishing)
- they exploit data leaks

Often, that is enough.

---

## Essential good practices

- use a **password manager**
- create unique passwords
- enable multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- stay alert to suspicious messages

---

## Takeaway

Security starts with simple habits.
A good password and a little vigilance can prevent serious consequences.
