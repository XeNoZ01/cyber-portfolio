Experience

Two internships and a volunteer role: GRC, system administration, networking.

This page presents the experiences that shaped my path in cybersecurity, on the technical side as well as on governance, risk and compliance.

Brainframe Technologies

Final-year internship – Governance, Risk and Compliance

March – August 2024 (6 months) · Gonderange, Junglinster (Luxembourg)

Brainframe Technologies publishes a governance, risk and compliance platform. During my final-year internship I led, under the supervision of its director, the ISO 27001 compliance project of a client company. That work was the subject of my internship and I carried out most of it. The client company obtained ISO 27001 certification.

This experience gave me hands-on knowledge of how an ISMS works and of the steps needed to bring an organisation into compliance.

Work carried out

  • Writing security policies and procedures
  • Carrying out ISO 27005 risk analyses
  • Helping define the ISMS scope
  • Producing compliance documentation
  • Contributing to audit preparation
  • Drawing up risk treatment plans
  • Taking part in continuous improvement activities

Business continuity and incident management

As part of the project, I also helped design:

  • BCP (Business Continuity Plan)
  • DRP (Disaster Recovery Plan)
  • IRP (Incident Response Plan)

Working method

Agile environment:

  • Sprints
  • Reviews
  • Retrospectives
  • Asana
  • Slack

What it gave me

This assignment confirmed my interest in governance and risk management.

It helped me understand how regulatory and normative requirements turn into concrete measures for companies.
