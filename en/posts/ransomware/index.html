Why ransomware remains a major threat

Published 2026-01-28 · 1 min read

Context

Ransomware attacks keep rising and hit large companies as well as small businesses, hospitals and local authorities.

Despite better awareness, this type of attack remains one of the most profitable for cybercriminals.

What is happening

Attackers mainly exploit:

  • unpatched vulnerabilities
  • services exposed on the Internet
  • human error (phishing, weak passwords)

Once access is gained, data encryption is often preceded by data theft, which increases the pressure on victims.

Why it matters

Ransomware is no longer only a technical problem:

  • it affects business continuity
  • it raises legal and regulatory issues
  • it damages the trust of customers and partners

Risk management becomes as important as the technical response.

Concrete impact for organisations

  • service interruption
  • financial loss
  • damage to reputation
  • risk of regulatory penalties

Preparation (backups, procedures, awareness) is often the deciding factor.

Takeaway

Ransomware illustrates the importance of an overall approach:

  • clear governance
  • suitable technical measures
  • aware users

Cybersecurity is not only about “preventing an attack”, but about limiting its impact.
